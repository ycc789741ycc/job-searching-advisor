I hope to design an app which could help users to revise their resume and setup the goal to improve career skills from their real work (import data from portfolio or project platform) for better hunting next jobs. The app is able to analyze user's experience, career trajectory and generate the report about user's skill strength with radar chart. Mean while, this app could analyze the most fit roles, reasonable salary range from job market platform with bubble chart. The app could also help user to revise the resume by the role and company that user targeting if the role user choosed has too much gap, the app is able to recommend the goal / skill that user needs to improve.
